Hello, I'm Yihan Liu (刘一涵), currently a PhD candidate at the School of Advanced Technology, Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU), supervised by Prof. Yu Liu. Before that, I received my MSc in Applied Computational Science and Engineering from Imperial College London and my BSc in Applied Mathematics from XJTLU and the University of Liverpool.

My research interests lie in human-computer interaction, interactive systems, multi-device interaction, smartwatch interaction, and mobile interaction. I am particularly interested in designing and evaluating interactive systems that make interaction across multiple devices more seamless and effective. My PhD research focuses on how smartwatches can complement other devices within multi-device systems to support more accessible and engaging user experiences.
